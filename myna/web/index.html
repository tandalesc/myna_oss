Pick up a phone.

A keypad phone that runs inside this page. It gets a number of its own, so you can text anyone else who has picked one up. Press its keys, slide it open, write a text: it comes back to you.

Size

The phone is drawn in 3D: the slide and the lid move as they do in the hand, and a sideways drag turns it round. The flat drawing asks less of the graphics chip.

Adjust the true size

Hold the short side of a bank card (54 mm) against the bar and move the slider until they match.

Key feedback

No tick? On the iPhone, open Settings, Sounds & Haptics, and turn on System Haptics.

Full screen

Safari on an iPhone has no full-screen button. Tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”, and open the page from its new icon: it then fills the display. The home-screen copy keeps its own storage, so the phone's software is downloaded once more there.
